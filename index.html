Section 01

DC’s Wine Country® Brand

DC’s Wine Country is Visit Loudoun’s flagship brand, and wineries are the top tourism draw to Loudoun. Partners are encouraged to use the brand, the logos and the messaging below in their own marketing.

Brand Study

A study of the DC’s Wine Country® brand conducted by Visit Loudoun and SMARInsights showed the county’s reputation as a wine destination resonates with visitors and places Loudoun’s wine region on the map alongside other nationally known wine destinations. Wineries are the top tourism draw to Loudoun.

Take advantage of these results and continue to build brand recognition by using the logos and messaging below on your website, social media and other marketing collateral.

Brand Logos

Visit Loudoun logo with the DC’s Wine Country® tagline

The Visit Loudoun logo must be used as is and cannot be modified under any circumstances without permission.

DC’s Wine Country® seal logo

There is only one DC’s Wine Country. As the home of more than 50 wineries and vineyards, what sets Loudoun apart is a wine region set among small towns and villages, rich history and scenic backdrops.

Designed to work with the main Visit Loudoun standard logo, the DC’s Wine Country seal can also be used as a standalone graphic element on photos and solid colors.

Tagline usage

In the logo, the registered trademark symbol must accompany “DC’s Wine Country®” in all instances with no exceptions. In copy, the symbol should be included on first reference — in a headline or in body copy — within each individual piece of content or ad unit.

Maintaining the integrity of the logo is vital to keeping the brand consistent. The logo must always be used in the formats and proportions provided.

Ways to Use the Brand

  • Use #DCsWineCountry in applicable social media posts.
  • Use “DC’s Wine Country®” on your website and on your social media channels.

    Example: “We are located in Loudoun, Virginia — DC’s Wine Country®”

  • Partner with local businesses to create packaged experiences for DC’s Wine Country®.

    Example: partner with a local bed & breakfast to offer a sip-and-stay package.

Love Loudoun Wedding Showcase

The Love Loudoun Wedding Showcase spotlights Loudoun’s community of wedding professionals and venues, bringing together all that makes Loudoun the “Go-To” destination for weddings. The high-touch event features a VIP Hour, Fashion Show, The Lounge and more.

Certified Tourism Ambassador (CTA) Program

The Loudoun CTA program is a certification program designed to increase tourism’s positive impact in the county and build a strong brand and destination image. Through this program, frontline employees, ambassadors and volunteers improve their knowledge of Loudoun, share best practices and develop a better understanding of their role in increasing tourism.
